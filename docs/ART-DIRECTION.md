# +1 Gravity — Art Direction

## Visual identity
Readable arcade sci-fi grounded in ordinary objects. The visual joke is escalation: a soda can orbit becomes a bicycle, car, container and finally building-scale debris.

## Palette
- Foundation: deep navy / charcoal.
- Primary energy: electric cyan.
- Secondary energy: violet.
- Reward: warm gold.
- Success: mint green.
- Danger/locked: muted red only when necessary.

## World
Use clean low-poly geometry with strong silhouettes and materially distinct zones. Avoid default baseplate aesthetics.

## Orbit
- 10–16 visible representative objects maximum.
- Objects occupy rings with different radii/heights.
- Small items merge into representative visuals rather than rendering every collected item.
- No physical constraints; client animates anchored/non-collidable visuals.
- Orbit must remain behind/around the avatar and leave the central character readable.

## UI
Mobile-first compact top status bar and bottom-right Collapse action. Avoid giant stacked cards. Keep the playfield open.

## Collapse
Orbit accelerates, energy condenses, quick white/cyan flash, objects disappear toward a singularity, Core reward presentation, immediate reset into a visibly faster next run.
